Inbox Triage

Deterministic, auditable email routing for shared inboxes. Not an LLM wrapper — a transparent rules engine that shows you which word triggered which decision.

Live demo Source (MIT)

The problem

A shared info@ gets every enquiry and nobody owns any of them. Published SME analyses put a 12-person firm's triage at roughly 375 hours a year — about CHF 22,500 at blended admin rates — before counting the quote request that went to a competitor who simply answered sooner.

It fails closed

A category-independent gate runs before any routing decision. Legal notices, complaints, billing disputes and security incidents are detected and held for a human, always.

This is the most important decision in the codebase, and it exists because v1 got it wrong: it classified first and checked sensitivity afterwards, so a legal notice phrased in sales language was routed as a sales enquiry and would have been auto-actioned. An adversarial regression case covers exactly that input.

It also abstains. Below a confidence threshold it returns unclassified and hands off to a person rather than guessing — because a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest one.

What it will never do

  • Send anything — it drafts, a human sends.
  • Auto-answer a complaint, legal notice, billing dispute or security incident.
  • Call a third-party API or train a model. Local only, content never leaves the machine.
